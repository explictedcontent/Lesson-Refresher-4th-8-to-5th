Build a Value Scale

Value means how light or dark something is. Artists use a value scale to practice every step from white to black. Pick a gray below, then click a box to fill it. Fill all 5 boxes so they go from lightest on the left to darkest on the right — then press Check!

Your value scale (light → dark)

Light the Sphere 🔦

Drag the sun around. Watch how the bright spot (highlight) and the dark side (shadow) move to the opposite side, and how the cast shadow stretches across the ground. Light always comes from one direction — that's what makes a ball look round!

Shade Your Own Ball

Press “Show guide” for a circle, a ground line, and a sun showing the light direction. Shade it like a 3D ball: leave a white highlight near the sun, get gradually darker toward the far side (the core shadow), and add a soft cast shadow on the ground. Use the grays — press hard for dark, light for light!
