Phase 2 Strategy Review 8 min

Today's skill: Multi-Step Word Problems with Division

U — Understand

Read the problem twice. Underline the question. Circle important numbers. Cross out information you don’t need.

P — Plan

Decide which operation(s) to use: +, −, ×, or ÷? Write a number sentence with a box for the unknown.

S — Solve

Do the math step by step. Show your work. Label Step 1 and Step 2 clearly for multi-step problems.

S — Check

Does your answer make sense? Use the opposite operation to verify. Re-read the question to make sure you answered it.

Key Words to Watch:
÷ Divide: share equally, split, per, how many in each, groups of
× Multiply: each, every, times, per, groups of
+ Add: total, combined, together, in all, sum, more than
− Subtract: difference, how many more, left, remaining, fewer

Worked Example — Step by Step

“A box holds 24 apples. If you want to share them equally among 4 friends, how many does each friend get? If each friend then gives away 3 apples, how many does each friend have left?”
U — Understand
Know: 24 apples total, 4 friends, then each gives away 3.
Find: How many apples does each friend have left?
P — Plan
Step 1: Share equally → use ÷
Step 2: Give away some → use −
S — Solve
Step 1: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 apples each shared equally
Step 2: 6 − 3 = 3 apples left gave away 3
S — Check
4 friends × 3 apples = 12 left total; 4 × 3 given = 12; 12 + 12 = 24 ✓
Answer: Each friend has 3 apples left.
