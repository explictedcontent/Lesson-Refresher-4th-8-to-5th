📐 Math — Day 3: Multi-Step Word Problems

Strategy · Word Problems · Mental Math · Quiz  |  45 minutes

Use the U-P-S-S method for any word problem — it works every time!

🔍

U — Understand

Read the problem twice. Underline the question. Circle important numbers. Cross out information you don't need.

📝

P — Plan

Decide which operation(s) to use: add, subtract, multiply, or divide? Write a number sentence with a box for the unknown.

✏️

S — Solve

Do the math step by step. Show your work. If the problem has two steps, label Step 1 and Step 2 clearly.

✅

S — Check

Does your answer make sense? Use the opposite operation to check. Re-read the question to make sure you answered it.

💡 Key Words to Watch:
Add (+): in all, total, combined, together, sum, more than
Subtract (−): difference, less than, how many more, left over, remaining
Multiply (×): each, every, times, product, groups of, per
Divide (÷): share equally, split, per, how many in each, quotient

Type your answer and tap Check. Show your work on scratch paper!
