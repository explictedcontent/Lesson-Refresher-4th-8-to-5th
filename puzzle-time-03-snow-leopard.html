🐆 I am a ghost with spots, yet no one calls me a spirit. I roam the highest peaks where eagles barely visit. I hunt alone, I leap like wind, my tail is long and pale. What am I?
A Snow Leopard 🐆
🌿 I can be a verb — I help you vanish. I can be a noun — I make armies invisible. Animals wear me without knowing it. What am I?
Camouflage 🎨
⛰️ I am the roof of Central Asia. 12 countries share my peaks. I am home to the ghost cat and the red panda. My tallest point is the highest on Earth. What am I?
The Himalayan Mountain Range ⛰️ (and nearby ranges like the Hindu Kush and Karakoram)
🔢 I am an even number. Divide me by 4 and you get 10. Multiply me by 3 and you get 120. What number am I?
40 (40 ÷ 4 = 10 ✓ and 40 × 3 = 120 ✓)
🦅 I sit at the top and eat everything below me, but nothing eats me. Remove me and the whole system tips. I am not a king but I rule the mountain. What am I?
An Apex Predator 🦁 (like the snow leopard!)
🌍 I have 12 owners but belong to all. I stretch from Russia to India. Countries share me but I belong to no single one. I am a strip of high mountains crossing a continent. What am I?
The Snow Leopard's Range — 12 countries across Central Asia 🗺️
🔤 I have 5 letters. Remove my first two and I become something you row on water. Keep all 5 and I describe something in danger of disappearing. What word am I?
"Vulnerable" — remove "vulne" and... hmm. Actually: "At risk" → the word is VULNERABLE. Remove first letter V → "ulnerable" — not quite. Fun approach: 5-letter hint applies to ELUDE → ELUSIVE! Remove first 2 letters "EL" → "USIVE." Or think: the word that means "in danger" with 10 letters is VULNERABLE. The 5-letter word in danger = "RISKY" → remove first 2 = "SKY" ✈️. Answer: RISKY (or accept: vulnerable!)
🧮 A snow leopard sleeps 18 hours a day. In a week, how many total hours is it AWAKE? (7 days in a week)
42 hours awake! (24 − 18 = 6 hours awake per day × 7 days = 42 hours)

🔍 Logic Puzzle: Mountain Wildlife Center

A wildlife center cares for 4 rescued animals: a Snow Leopard, a Red Panda, an Ibex, and a Golden Eagle. Each animal has a different enclosure number (1, 2, 3, 4) and eats a different food (Meat, Bamboo, Grass, Fish). Use the clues to figure out which animal is in which enclosure and eats what!
Clues:
  1. The Snow Leopard is in an odd-numbered enclosure.
  2. The Red Panda eats bamboo and is NOT in enclosure 1.
  3. The animal in enclosure 4 eats fish.
  4. The Ibex eats grass and is in enclosure 2.
  5. The Golden Eagle is not in enclosure 1 or 3.
  6. The Snow Leopard does not eat fish or grass.
