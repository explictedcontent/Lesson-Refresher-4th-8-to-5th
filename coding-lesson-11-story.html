Build Step 1 of 4
🔤 Words in a Variable
A variable can hold TEXT, not just numbers. Text in code is called a STRING (it goes in quotes). Type your name and watch it drop into the sentence live!
Hello, my name is ??? and I love to code! 🐼
let name = "???"; // a string = text in quotes say("Hello, my name is " + name);
A string is just text stored in a variable — always wrapped in quotes like "panda". Numbers or words, a variable can hold either. Now your name lives inside the code!
Build Step 2 of 4
🔗 Glue Words Together
The + sign glues strings together — that's called joining, or "concatenation." Fill both boxes and watch the code snap your words into one sentence!
The ??? ??? jumped over the moon! 🌙
let sentence = "The " + color + " " + animal + " jumped over the moon!";
Concatenation is a big word for gluing strings with +. You even add spaces (" ") so the words don't smush together. This is how programs build any message out of pieces.
Build Step 3 of 4
📝 Fill in the Blanks
Here's the Mad Libs trick: a sentence with BLANKS, and each blank is a variable. Fill in the words, hit Build, and your words slot right in!
Each blank is a variable waiting for a word. The template stays the same, but the words change — so the same code can make a MILLION different sentences. That's the whole idea of a story machine!
📝
Your words filled the blanks!
Build Step 4 of 4
📖 A Whole Template
A story template is a long string with lots of blanks. Fill them all, press Generate, and the machine writes a whole silly paragraph with YOUR words!
You just ran a real template: fixed words plus blanks that get filled from variables. Websites, games, and text messages build almost everything this way — a template + your data = a finished message.

A whole story from your words!
