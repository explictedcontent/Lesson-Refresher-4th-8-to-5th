📋 Worked Example (clean numbers)

A store sold 240 items. One-third were returned. How many items were kept?

U Know: 240 items sold, 1/3 returned. Find: items kept.
P Find 1/3 of 240, then subtract from 240. Or find 2/3 directly.
S 240 ÷ 3 = 80 returned  →  240 − 80 = 160 kept.
S Check: 80 + 160 = 240 ✓. Makes sense!
