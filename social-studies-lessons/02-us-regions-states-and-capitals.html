U.S. Regions, States & Capitals

The United States is one big country split into 5 regions, each with its own land, weather, and famous places. In this lesson you'll explore all five, learn cool landmarks and states, practice using a map and a compass, and crack the trick that the capital is not always the biggest city. Then you'll prove what you know with a giant bank of problems.

⏱ 30 minutes 🧩 50+ problems 🧭 Map & compass skills 4th → 5th grade
Peek at the 5 U.S. regions (you'll explore these in the lesson)
For the grown-up: Built for a real 30 minutes that won't run short. A timer breaks the lesson into six steps, and the Quiz, Rapid Round, and Bonus pull from a pool of 50+ problems, so a fast learner always has more to do — the Bonus Challenge can stretch it past 40 minutes. The level is kept friendly for a 10-year-old: well-known states, landmarks, and just a few key capitals. Read the explanations out loud and connect back to the big idea from Lesson 1: the land and climate shape how people live and work.
