How the U.S. Government Works — The 3 Branches

Our country's government is split into three branches so that no single person or group ever gets too much power. One branch makes the laws, one carries them out, and one decides what they mean. In this lesson you'll meet all three branches, learn how they keep each other fair (checks and balances), and prove what you know with a giant bank of problems.
