Native American Cultures & Regions

Long before the United States existed, hundreds of Native nations lived across North America. Where they lived — the land, the weather, the animals, and the plants — shaped how they lived: their homes, their food, their clothes, and how they traveled. In this lesson you'll explore five culture regions and prove what you know with a big bank of problems.
