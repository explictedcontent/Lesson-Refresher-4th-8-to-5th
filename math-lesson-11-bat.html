The clock above shows 1:00. The short hand points at the 1 (the hour), and the long hand points straight up at the 12 (0 minutes). To read minutes, count by 5s around the clock: the 3 means 15 minutes, the 6 means 30 minutes, and the 9 means 45 minutes. A day has two rounds of the clock: AM for morning and PM for afternoon and night.

📈 Phase 3 — Elapsed Time (10 min)

Elapsed time is how much time passes from a START time to an END time. Count forward — hours first, then minutes.

📋 Counting Forward on a Time Number Line

Worked example: how long is it from 2:15 to 4:45?

The Two-Step Count
2:15 → 4:15 is 2 hours 4:15 → 4:45 is 30 minutes Total elapsed = 2 hours 30 minutes
Jump by whole hours first to get close, then count the leftover minutes.
Another way: to add 90 minutes to a time, remember 90 minutes = 1 hour 30 minutes. So 4:00 + 90 minutes lands on 5:30. Always split minutes over 60 into hours + minutes.
