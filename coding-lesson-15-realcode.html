🧠 Start here — how this works
You write commands in the box. A command is just an order for the panda. This one means “take one step to the right”: 
right()
Three rules, and that’s honestly it:
1️⃣ Put one command per line.
2️⃣ The computer reads your lines top to bottom, like a to-do list.
3️⃣ Press ▶ RUN and watch it happen. Wrong? Just fix it and RUN again — that’s what real coders do all day.

Your commands (each moves the panda ONE square — it grabs any bamboo it lands on):

right()left()up()down()say("hi")

💬 Lines starting with // are notes to yourself. The computer skips them — they’re just there to explain things.
