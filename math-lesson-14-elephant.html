⚖️ Phase 3 — Rounding & Comparing (10 min)

Round to a tidy number, and line up places to see which is bigger.

📋 Rounding — Look at the Digit to the Right

To round, find your place, then peek at the digit just to its right. If it is 5 or more, round up; if it is 4 or less, round down. Rounding 3,847 to the nearest hundred:

3,847 is left of the halfway mark (3,850), and its tens digit 4 is less than 5 → rounds down to 3,800.

Watch out: 62,500 to the nearest thousand — the hundreds digit is exactly 5, so it rounds up to 63,000. Half or more always rounds up.
