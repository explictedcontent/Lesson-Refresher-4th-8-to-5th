Lesson 1 — Place Value: Math Lab 🔢

Summer Math · Going into 5th Grade · about 30 minutes

How to play: tap digits, build numbers, and answer to earn XP. Get one right on the first try to grow your 🔥 streak. Earn all 7 badges to become Champion! Everything saves on this tablet automatically.

0–4 min Warm-Up: Tap the Digit

No typing here — just tap the right box. Each place is a different color.

Say it out loud too: read 309 ("three hundred nine") and 1,250 ("one thousand, two hundred fifty"). No typing — just say them to your grown-up.

4–10 min Teach: How Place Value Works

Every digit has two things: the digit itself, and the place it sits in. The place tells you what it's worth. Each place to the left is 10× bigger than the one to its right.

Millions | Hund-Thous | Ten-Thous | Thousands | Hundreds | Tens | Ones
1,000,000   100,000     10,000      1,000        100      10      1

We split big numbers into periods of three, separated by commas: 3,205,070 reads "three million, two hundred five thousand, seventy." Read to a comma, say "million," read to the next comma, say "thousand," then the rest.

16–21 min ×10 / ÷10 Power ⚡ (big 5th-grade idea)

Moving one place to the left makes a digit worth 10 times as much. Moving one place to the right makes it one-tenth (÷10) as much. Try it:

28–30 min 👑 Boss: Mystery Number

Crack the code. It's a 5-digit number where…
