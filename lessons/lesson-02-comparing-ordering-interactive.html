5–10 min Teach: How to Compare Numbers

To compare two numbers, use this 3-step trick:

  1. Count the digits. More digits = bigger number (line them up by place value).
  2. Same number of digits? Compare from the left — the highest place first.
  3. Stop at the first place where the digits differ. The bigger digit there wins. ✋
  6,481      Same number of digits (4 each).
  6,491      Thousands match (6=6). Hundreds match (4=4).
  ^          Tens: 8 vs 9 → 9 wins.  So 6,491 > 6,481.
Mouth trick: the < or > sign is a hungry mouth — it always opens toward the bigger number. 25 < 50 (the mouth eats the 50). Read left to right: 25 < 50 = "25 is less than 50."
