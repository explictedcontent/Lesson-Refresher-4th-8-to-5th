5–15 min Teach: How to compare numbers

To compare two numbers, don't just look at how many digits "feel" big. Use this 3-step trick:

  1. Line them up by place value (ones under ones, tens under tens…). The number with more digits is bigger.
  2. If they have the same number of digits, compare from the left — the highest place first.
  3. Stop at the first place where the digits are different. The bigger digit there wins. ✋
  6,481      Same number of digits (4 each).
  6,491      Thousands match (6=6). Hundreds match (4=4).
  ^          Tens: 8 vs 9 → 9 wins.  So 6,491 > 6,481.
Mouth trick: the < or > sign is a hungry mouth — it always opens toward the bigger number. 25 < 50 (mouth eats the 50).

Reading the symbol the right direction

You read left to right. 8 > 3 says "8 is greater than 3." 3 < 8 says "3 is less than 8." Same fact, flipped.

Ordering a list

Least to greatest = smallest first. Greatest to least = biggest first. Compare two at a time using the trick above until the whole list is in order.
