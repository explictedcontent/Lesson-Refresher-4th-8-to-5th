4–9 min Teach: Line Up & Regroup

To add or subtract, line the numbers up by place value (ones under ones, tens under tens…) and work right to left, one column at a time.

ADD — carry when a column is 10 or more:
   ¹ ¹
   4 7 8        8+5 = 13 → write 3, carry 1
 + 3 4 5        7+4+1 = 12 → write 2, carry 1
 -------        4+3+1 = 8
 8 2 3
SUBTRACT — borrow when the top digit is too small:
   4 ¹0 ¹2      can't do 2−8, borrow: 12−8 = 4
   5̶ 1̶ 2        0−7 → borrow: 10−7 = 3 ... wait, line up & go slow!
 - 2 7 8
 -------
 2 3 4
Smart check: before you compute, estimate by rounding each number. 512 − 278 ≈ 500 − 300 = 200, so an answer near 200 makes sense. If your real answer is wildly different, you made a regrouping slip — go back and check.
