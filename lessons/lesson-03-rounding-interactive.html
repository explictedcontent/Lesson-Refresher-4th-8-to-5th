4–9 min Teach: How to Round

Rounding makes a number simpler by snapping it to the nearest ten, hundred, thousand, and so on. Here's the 4-step rule:

  1. Find the place you're rounding to. Underline it.
  2. Look at the digit just to its RIGHT — only that one.
  3. If it's 0–4, round down (the place digit stays the same). If it's 5–9, round up (the place digit goes up by 1).
  4. Every digit to the right of the place becomes 0.
Round 6,482 to the nearest THOUSAND.
  6,482   place = thousands (6). Digit to the right = 4.
  ^       4 is 0–4 → round DOWN → 6,000.

Round 7,650 to the nearest THOUSAND.
  7,650   place = thousands (7). Digit to the right = 6.
  ^       6 is 5–9 → round UP → 8,000.
Number-line trick: ask "which one is it closer to?" 6,482 sits between 6,000 and 7,000 — it's closer to 6,000. Exactly halfway (like x,500) always rounds up.
