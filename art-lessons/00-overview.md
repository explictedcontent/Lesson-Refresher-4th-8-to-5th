# Art Refresher Course — 4th Grade into 5th Grade

A 14-lesson summer refresher to keep art skills sharp between 4th and 5th grade.
The course reviews the **7 Elements of Art** and the **Principles of Design**, then
builds toward drawing skills (perspective, proportion, portraits) and a capstone project.

## Why these lessons run a full 30 minutes

Yesterday's lesson finished in 15 minutes. Every lesson here is built to fill **30 minutes**
with a clear minute-by-minute breakdown, AND each one includes an **"If You Finish Early"**
extension challenge so a fast learner always has more to do. If 30 minutes still isn't enough,
the extension can stretch a lesson to 40–45 minutes.

## How each lesson is structured

| Section | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up | 5 min | A quick sketch or game to loosen up and review |
| Concept / Mini-Lesson | 7 min | The new idea, with vocabulary |
| Guided Practice | 6 min | Try it together, step by step |
| Main Activity | 10 min | Create the lesson's project |
| Reflection / Share | 2 min | Talk about what worked |
| **If You Finish Early** | +10–15 min | Bonus challenge so no one runs out of work |

## Materials used across the course (gather once)

- Pencils (regular + colored), eraser, pencil sharpener
- Markers or crayons
- Plain white paper / sketchbook
- Black fine-tip marker or Sharpie
- Ruler
- Optional: watercolor or tempera paint, glue, scissors, old magazines

## The 14 lessons

1. **Line** — types of lines and expressive lines
2. **Shape & Form** — 2D shapes vs. 3D forms
3. **Color Wheel** — primary, secondary, tertiary colors
4. **Color Moods** — warm vs. cool, tints & shades
5. **Value & Shading** — light to dark, making things look round
6. **Texture** — real texture vs. implied texture
7. **Space** — positive/negative space, foreground & background
8. **One-Point Perspective** — making flat paper look deep
9. **Balance & Symmetry** — symmetrical and asymmetrical designs
10. **Pattern & Rhythm** — repeating elements with purpose
11. **Emphasis & Focal Point** — making one thing stand out
12. **Proportion & Portraits** — drawing a face that looks right
13. **Art History & Styles** — famous artists and how to "borrow" a style
14. **Capstone Project** — combine everything into one finished artwork

## Tips for the teacher / parent

- Read the lesson once before starting so you can demo each step.
- It's fine to slow down — quality over speed. The timer is a guide, not a rule.
- Praise effort and choices ("I like how you used a warm color there"), not just "nice."
- Keep finished work in a folder so you can see growth across the 14 lessons.
