# Lesson 13: Art History & Styles

**Topic:** Famous artists, art movements, and "borrowing" a style
**Time:** 30 minutes

## Learning goal
The student can name a few famous artists and movements, describe what makes each style
special, and create their own work in one chosen style.

## Vocabulary
- **Style** — the special "look" an artist or group is known for.
- **Movement** — a group of artists who shared ideas during a time period.
- **A few to know:**
  - **Vincent van Gogh** — *Post-Impressionism*; thick, swirling brushstrokes (*The Starry Night*).
  - **Pablo Picasso** — *Cubism*; objects broken into shapes and shown from many angles at once.
  - **Claude Monet** — *Impressionism*; soft dabs of color, capturing light and moments.
  - **Henri Matisse** — bold colors and cut-paper shapes.
  - **Georgia O'Keeffe** — giant close-up flowers and desert scenes.
  - **Jackson Pollock** — *Abstract Expressionism*; dripped and splattered paint.
  - **Piet Mondrian** — grids of straight lines with red, yellow, blue squares.

## Materials
- White paper
- Markers, crayons, colored pencils, or paint
- Ruler (for the Mondrian option)

## Lesson plan (30 minutes)

**Warm-Up — 5 min**
Look at (or recall) *The Starry Night*. Describe it in three words. Try a quick patch of
"swirly" sky in van Gogh's style — short curved strokes that move like wind.

**Concept / Mini-Lesson — 7 min**
Walk through 3–4 of the artists above. For each, ask: "How would I recognize this artist?"
Point out that styles are like handwriting — each artist sees and shows the world
differently. Connect to earlier lessons: Mondrian = line + primary color + balance;
Pollock = rhythm + movement; Picasso = shape + space.

**Guided Practice — 6 min**
Pick ONE artist to imitate. Talk through the key "rules" of that style (e.g., Mondrian:
only straight black lines, only fill some boxes with red/yellow/blue, leave the rest white).

**Main Activity — 10 min: "In the Style Of…"**
Make your own small artwork in the chosen artist's style:
- **Van Gogh:** a swirling night sky over a little town.
- **Mondrian:** a grid of black lines with a few primary-color boxes.
- **Matisse/Picasso:** a face or still life from simple bold shapes.
- **Pollock:** a controlled splatter/drip design (do this one on a tray or outdoors!).

**Reflection / Share — 2 min**
What makes your artwork look like that artist? What did you learn about their choices?

## If You Finish Early (+10–15 min)
**Two-Style Mashup:** Combine two artists — for example, a Mondrian grid where each box is
filled with a tiny van Gogh swirl, or a Picasso-style face made of Matisse cut-paper
shapes. Write one sentence about why you combined them.

## What to look for (teacher check)
- Student can name at least 3 artists and one thing each is known for.
- The artwork clearly reflects the chosen style's "rules."
