# Lesson 8: One-Point Perspective

**Skill:** Linear perspective (builds on Space)
**Time:** 30 minutes

## Learning goal
The student can set up a horizon line and a vanishing point and draw a room or a road
that looks like it goes back into deep space.

## Vocabulary
- **Perspective** — a way of drawing 3D depth on flat paper.
- **Horizon line** — the line where the sky meets the ground; your eye level.
- **Vanishing point** — the single dot on the horizon where parallel lines seem to meet.
- **Orthogonal lines** — the "guide" lines that run from objects back to the vanishing point.

## Materials
- Pencil, eraser, and a **ruler** (important today)
- White paper
- Black marker for final lines

## Lesson plan (30 minutes)

**Warm-Up — 5 min**
Look down a hallway or a long straight road (or a photo of one). Notice how the edges seem
to tilt toward a single far-away point. Draw a quick 30-second sketch of that effect.

**Concept / Mini-Lesson — 7 min**
Draw a horizon line across the paper and put ONE dot on it — the vanishing point. Explain:
every edge that runs *away* from us aims at that dot. Things look smaller as they get
closer to the point.

**Guided Practice — 6 min**
Together, draw a simple box in one-point perspective:
1. Draw a square (the front of the box).
2. Lightly draw lines from each corner to the vanishing point.
3. Draw a smaller square behind it to "stop" the box.
4. Trace the edges you can see; erase the extra guide lines.

**Main Activity — 10 min: "Road or Room"**
Choose one:
- **Road:** A road that narrows to the vanishing point, with telephone poles or trees that
  get smaller as they go back.
- **Room:** A box room — back wall is a rectangle around the vanishing point; floor,
  ceiling, and side walls drawn with orthogonal lines. Add a door and window using the
  guide lines so they sit correctly in space.

**Reflection / Share — 2 min**
Where is your vanishing point? Does the scene look like it goes back into the distance?

## If You Finish Early (+10–15 min)
**Name in Perspective:** Write your name in block letters, then send every corner back to
the vanishing point and close off the depth. Your name becomes a row of 3D blocks. Shade
the side and top of each letter for extra effect.

## What to look for (teacher check)
- All depth lines aim at the SAME vanishing point.
- Objects shrink correctly as they move toward the point.
