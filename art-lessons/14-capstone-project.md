# Lesson 14: Capstone Project — Put It All Together

**Topic:** A finished artwork using everything from the course
**Time:** 30 minutes (can extend over 2 sessions)

## Learning goal
The student can plan and create one finished artwork that uses several elements of art and
principles of design on purpose, and can explain their choices.

## Review checklist (the whole course)
**Elements of Art:** Line · Shape · Form · Color · Value · Texture · Space
**Principles of Design:** Balance · Pattern/Rhythm · Emphasis · Proportion · Perspective

## Materials
- Best paper available
- All the tools used so far: pencils, colored pencils, markers/crayons, ruler, black marker,
  paint if available
- The color wheel and value scale made earlier (keep them out as references)

## Lesson plan (30 minutes)

**Warm-Up — 5 min: Plan It**
Pick a subject you care about (a pet, a dream treehouse, an underwater world, a city at
night). Make a quick thumbnail sketch — small and fast — to plan the layout. Decide your
focal point now.

**Concept / Mini-Lesson — 5 min: Choose Your Tools**
From the checklist, choose at least **FOUR** things to use on purpose. Write them at the
top of your planning page. Example: *one-point perspective + warm colors + a clear focal
point + texture on the foreground object.*

**Main Activity — 18 min: Make It**
Draw the final piece lightly in pencil first (placement, perspective, proportions), then
add value, color, texture, and details. Keep checking your four chosen goals. Use the
color wheel for color choices and the value scale for shading.

**Reflection / Share — 2 min: Artist Statement**
On the back, finish these sentences:
- "My focal point is ______."
- "Four art ideas I used on purpose: ______."
- "The part I'm most proud of is ______."

## If You Finish Early (or for a second session) (+15+ min)
**Title, Frame & Gallery:** Give the artwork a title and sign it. Make a paper frame.
Then set up a mini "gallery walk" — line up the favorite piece from each of the 14 lessons
and look at how the skills grew from Lesson 1 to now.

## What to look for (teacher check)
- At least four elements/principles used intentionally.
- A clear focal point and a finished, considered piece.
- Student can talk about WHY they made their choices — that's the real win of the refresher.

---

🎉 **Course complete!** The student has reviewed all 7 elements of art and the core
principles of design and is ready for 5th-grade art. Keep the finished folder — it's a
great "before summer / after summer" record of growth.
