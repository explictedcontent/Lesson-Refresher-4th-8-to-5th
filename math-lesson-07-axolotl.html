📐 Phase 2 — Know Your Angles (8 min)

An angle is measured in degrees (°). There are 4 kinds you need to know.

📋 The Four Angle Types

Memory trick: a-CUTE angle is small and cute (less than 90°). A RIGHT angle makes a perfect corner, exactly 90° — like the corner of a book. OBTUSE angles are wide open (more than 90° but less than 180°). A STRAIGHT angle opens all the way flat — exactly 180°, a straight line.

📏 Phase 3 — Perimeter & Area (10 min)

Perimeter goes AROUND. Area COVERS. Learn both, then practice.

📋 Two Big Ideas

PERIMETER = distance around, like a fence around a yard.
Add ALL the sides: 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 units
AREA = space covered, like tiles covering a floor.
Length × width: 5 × 3 = 15 square units
Perimeter is measured in plain units (feet, meters). Area is measured in SQUARE units (square feet, square meters) — because you're counting little squares! For a square, all 4 sides are equal, so perimeter = 4 × side and area = side × side.
