Which is Closer? 0 / 0

On flat paper, artists use tricks to show space (near and far). Closer things look bigger, sit lower on the page, and can overlap (cover) the things behind them. Tap the tree that looks CLOSER to you!

Round 1

Positive or Negative Space?

Positive space = the OBJECT itself. Negative space = the empty area AROUND and between objects. Look at the teal-colored part — is it the object, or the space around it?
