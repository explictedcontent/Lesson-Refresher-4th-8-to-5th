The Red Panda

Read carefully — the teal words are your vocabulary words for today.

High in the misty mountains of Asia lives one of the world's most charming animals: the red panda. About the size of a house cat, it has rusty-red fur, a striped tail, and a face that looks like it's always smiling. The red panda is arboreal, which means it spends most of its life up in the trees. Its reddish coat is perfect camouflage among the red moss and pale lichen of its mountain habitat. Red pandas are crepuscular — most active at dawn and dusk, when the forest is cool and quiet. To survive the cold, a red panda wraps its bushy tail around its body like a furry blanket. Sadly, red pandas are endangered: as people cut down their forests, fewer wild red pandas remain. Protecting their mountain home is the best way to help them survive.
