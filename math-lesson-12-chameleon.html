✖️ Phase 3 — Multiply & Divide (10 min)

Use the standard algorithm: multiply place by place, or divide left to right.

📋 Two Worked Examples

2-digit × 1-digit: 24 × 3
2 4 × 3 ----- 7 2
3 × 4 = 12 → write 2, carry 1. 3 × 2 = 6, plus the carried 1 = 7. Answer: 72.
3-digit ÷ 1-digit: 936 ÷ 3
3 1 2 3)9 3 6
9 ÷ 3 = 3, then 3 ÷ 3 = 1, then 6 ÷ 3 = 2. Nothing left over. Answer: 312.
Check by reversing: multiplication and division undo each other. Since 24 × 3 = 72, you can check with 72 ÷ 3 = 24. Since 936 ÷ 3 = 312, check with 312 × 3 = 936.
