📏 Phase 2 — Unit Reference (8 min)

Learn the key conversions, then practice going from one unit to another.

📋 The Conversions You Need to Know

Length
10 millimeters (mm)=1 centimeter (cm)
100 centimeters (cm)=1 meter (m)
1,000 meters (m)=1 kilometer (km)
12 inches (in)=1 foot (ft)
3 feet (ft)=1 yard (yd)
Weight / Mass
1,000 grams (g)=1 kilogram (kg)
16 ounces (oz)=1 pound (lb)
Capacity
1,000 milliliters (mL)=1 liter (L)
Going from a BIG unit to a small unit, MULTIPLY (there are more small pieces). Example: 3 m → cm is 3 × 100 = 300 cm. Going from a small unit to a BIG unit, DIVIDE (it takes many small pieces to make one big one). Example: 500 cm → m is 500 ÷ 100 = 5 m.

⚖️ Phase 3 — Convert & Compare (10 min)

To compare two measurements fairly, change them to the SAME unit first.

📋 Compare by Matching the Units

You cannot compare different units head-to-head. First convert both to the same unit, then compare the numbers. Which is longer, 1 m or 90 cm?

Step 1 — Convert
1 m = 100 cm
Step 2 — Compare
100 cm > 90 cm
Answer
1 m is longer
Trap: 90 has a bigger number than 1, so it looks bigger — but the UNITS are different. Once both are in centimeters, 100 cm clearly beats 90 cm. Always match units before you judge.
