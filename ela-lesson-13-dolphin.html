Reading Passage
10 min

Finn Finds His Whistle

Finn was a young dolphin who did not yet have his own signature whistle — the special sound every dolphin uses like a name. All around him, the other young dolphins in the pod already had theirs. They chirped and called back and forth across the reef, each one answering to a sound that was truly its own. When they played, they could find each other in a heartbeat, no matter how far apart they drifted. Finn felt left out, and he wasn't sure he could ever make a whistle of his own.

"Why doesn't mine come out?" Finn asked his mother one evening. Marina swam close and nuzzled him gently. "Your whistle will come when you find your own voice," she said. "Don't just copy the others. A borrowed sound will never belong to you."

But Finn was impatient. He didn't want to wait for something to appear on its own. So he tried to imitate his friend Coral's bright, bubbly whistle. He practiced it again and again, day after day, but it always felt fake, like wearing a mask that did not fit his face. When he called out with Coral's sound, the other dolphins looked around, confused, searching for a friend who wasn't really there. No one ever answered him, and each time, Finn sank a little lower in the water.

Then one gray afternoon, the pod went hunting in a stretch of murky water. A cloud of silt swirled up, and suddenly Finn could not see anyone. He was alone, and his heart raced. He had to call out — but not with Coral's whistle, not with anyone's. In that urgent moment, without thinking, Finn let out a sound that was completely his own: clear, ringing, and distinct from every other voice in the sea.

The pod answered instantly. Whistles came rushing back from every direction, and moments later Marina appeared out of the gloom beside him. His true whistle had worked — not because it was loud, but because it was authentic. It was really his.

Finn understood now. His voice had been inside him all along. He hadn't needed to borrow anyone else's — he'd only needed to stop copying and be genuine. As the pod gathered around him, Finn felt a warm rush of confidence and a deep sense of belonging. He was proud, at last, to answer to a name that was truly and only his own.
