What kind of Balance? 0 / 0

Balance is how the "visual weight" is spread out so an artwork feels steady. Three kinds: Symmetrical (both sides match like a mirror), Asymmetrical (sides look different but still feel balanced), Radial (everything spreads out from a center point). Look and choose!

Round 1

Mirror Studio 🦋

Magic mirror drawing! In Symmetry mode, whatever you draw on one side appears on the other — perfect for a balanced butterfly or face. In Radial mode it repeats all the way around the center to make a mandala or snowflake. Your work auto-saves.
