🚀 Phase 2 — Rate & Speed (8 min)

A RATE tells how much of one thing happens in each unit of another — like miles in each hour.

📋 The Distance–Speed–Time Triangle

Find SPEED
speed = distance ÷ time
Find DISTANCE
distance = speed × time
Find TIME
time = distance ÷ speed
Cover the letter you want to find in the triangle. D is on top, so S and T sit side by side underneath. Cover D → you see S next to T → multiply: D = S × T. Cover S → you see D over T → divide: S = D ÷ T. Cover T → you see D over S → divide: T = D ÷ S.

✖️ Phase 3 — Multiply & Divide (10 min)

The standard algorithm lets you multiply and divide BIG numbers, one place at a time.

📋 Two Worked Examples

2-digit × 2-digit: 34 × 12
3 4 × 1 2 ------ 6 8 ← 34 × 2 3 4 0 ← 34 × 10 ------ 4 0 8
Multiply by the ones digit (2), then the tens digit (10). Add a 0 in the tens row, then add both lines: 68 + 340 = 408.
Long division: 156 ÷ 6
2 6 ----- 6 ) 1 5 6 -1 2 ---- 3 6 -3 6 ---- 0
6 goes into 15 two times (12), remainder 3. Bring down the 6 → 36. 6 goes into 36 six times. Answer: 26, no remainder.
For multiplication, line up the places and always add a 0 when you multiply by the tens digit. For division, work left to right: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — then repeat.
