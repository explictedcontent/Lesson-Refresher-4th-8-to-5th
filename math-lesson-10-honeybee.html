📊 Phase 2 — Reading a Bar Graph (8 min)

A bar graph turns numbers into bars. Taller bar = more. Always read the side axis to find the amount.

📋 How a Bar Graph Works

To read a bar: find its top, then slide your finger left to the number axis. Thursday's bar reaches 6, so 6 books were read Thursday. The tallest bar is the most; the shortest bar is the fewest. To compare two days, look at the difference in their heights.

📈 Phase 3 — Tally Charts & Line Plots (10 min)

A tally chart counts things quickly. A line plot stacks an X for each answer above a number line.

📋 The Same Data, Two Ways

A class was asked: "How many pets do you have?" Here are the results as a tally chart:

And the same counts drawn as a line plot — one X per person:

The tallest stack is above 1, so 1 pet is the MODE (most common). The values that appear go from 0 up to 3, so the RANGE is 3 − 0 = 3. Add every count (2 + 5 + 3 + 1) to find how many people were surveyed in total.
